## Procedure: hand work to the merge train or the deploy owner

Merging and deploying are work, so you never do them yourself. Who does them, and when, is set by the user's memories: follow those, and ask with a question item when a memory does not cover the case.

1. **Ready work** goes to whoever the memories name, with the PR as a full URL. Never merge or deploy yourself.
2. **Draft or gated work** is never handed on; file the go-ahead as a question item and wait.
3. **Report** in one line who has the work, linking the conversation.

A deployment can replace this page with its own rules: see `BRIDGE_COORDINATOR_OVERLAY_DIR` in the README.
